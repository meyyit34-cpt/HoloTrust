HoloTrust — Vision & Mission

1. Vision

Build an open, decentralized trust infrastructure for the agent-centric internet.

HoloTrust aims to make identity, reputation, and trust portable across applications, organizations, humans, AI agents, and digital services.

2. Mission

Create a Holochain-powered network where participants can:

* Control their own cryptographic identities.
* Publish and verify signed attestations.
* Build contextual, evidence-based reputations.
* Establish direct trust relationships.
* Interact without depending on a single central identity provider.

3. The Problem

Today’s digital trust systems are fragmented and centralized.

* Identity is controlled by platforms.
* Reputation is trapped inside individual services.
* Credentials are difficult to verify across ecosystems.
* AI agents introduce new questions about identity, authority, and accountability.

HoloTrust addresses these problems through decentralized, verifiable data.

4. Core Principles

Self-Sovereignty

Participants control their own keys and identity records.

Privacy by Design

Sensitive personal information must not be published to public DHT storage.

Verifiability

Attestations must have identifiable issuers, cryptographic signatures, and verifiable evidence references.

Contextual Reputation

Reputation is domain-specific and evidence-based. No universal trust score should define a participant.

Agent-Centric Design

Humans, AI agents, and services are first-class network participants.

Interoperability

Identity and attestations should be usable across independent applications.

5. Architecture Layers

1. Identity — cryptographic identity and profile.
2. Attestations — signed claims and credentials.
3. Reputation — contextual evaluation of evidence.
4. Trust Graph — relationships between participants.
5. Economic Layer — future integration with hREA and Holochain-based economic infrastructure.

6. Why Holochain?

Holochain’s agent-centric architecture provides a foundation for independently operated agents, source-chain integrity, and distributed validation.

HoloTrust will use Holochain as its distributed application framework rather than relying on a single global blockchain.

7. Security Boundaries

A public key proves control of a cryptographic key. It does not prove a person’s civil identity.

HoloTrust will not expose private keys, passwords, or unnecessary sensitive personal data.

Reputation claims must be distinguishable from verified facts.

8. Long-Term Goal

Become a reusable trust layer for the emerging agent-centric internet.

HoloTrust — Trust without centralized ownership.
